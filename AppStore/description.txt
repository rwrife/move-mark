Move Mark: Offline move-day box tracker for phone, tablet, and Windows.

Moving homes is stressful when you open the wrong carton looking for your kettle, phone charger, or work laptop. Move Mark gives you a private, searchable manifest of every box you pack—without accounts, cloud sync, or internet access.

FEATURES (Planned for initial release):
- Carton Manifest: Number each box, list its contents, note fragile items, and assign it to its destination room.
- Essentials & Priority: Flag unpack-first boxes so you immediately find night-one necessities and work tools.
- Box Lifecycle: Track status from Packed, to Loaded, to Delivered, to Opened, with reversible check-off.
- Search & Room Filter: Find any packed item in seconds across all cartons, or filter by destination room.
- Fully Offline & Private: All records stay on your device. No analytics, no accounts, and no trackers.
- Local Backup & Export: Export your manifest to CSV or JSON backup files, and restore anytime.
- Responsive & Platform-Authentic: Works seamlessly on phone, tablet, and Windows desktop with native-feeling controls and full keyboard support.

NOTE: Move Mark is a self-hosted, offline organization tool. It does not include automated shipping carrier tracking, barcode/QR scanning, or remote multi-user cloud synchronization.
